# Performance Based Distribution

Default Mode.

The system calculates Employee Performance Score.

Higher Score

↓

Receives more Leads.

Performance Score must NOT depend on reservations only.

Calculate from:

Lead Response Time

Lead Follow-up Compliance

Reservation Conversion Rate

Contract Conversion Rate

Sales Conversion Rate

Lost Lead Rate

Customer Follow-up Quality

Task Completion

Attendance Score (from HR)

Punctuality (from HR)

Disciplinary Actions (from HR)

Manager Evaluation (optional)

Training Completion (from HR)

Current Active Leads

Employee Availability

Vacation Status

Maximum Active Leads Limit

Then calculate

Performance Score = 0-100

Automatically calculate Lead Distribution Percentage.
