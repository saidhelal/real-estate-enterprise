Implement a global ERP extensibility and configuration framework across the entire system.

This is not a new module. It is a system-wide enhancement that must apply to all existing and future ERP modules.

Requirements:

1. Build a centralized Master Data & Configuration Engine.

2. Remove all hardcoded business values wherever possible and replace them with configurable data managed from the ERP.

3. Create a System Administration area containing:

   * Master Data Management
   * Dynamic Lists
   * System Configuration
   * Business Settings
   * Roles & Permissions

4. Administrators must be able to create, edit, activate, deactivate, archive, reorder and manage:

   * Categories
   * Types
   * Statuses
   * Classifications
   * Departments
   * Job Titles
   * Customer Types
   * Lead Sources
   * Unit Types
   * Unit Statuses
   * Contract Types
   * Payment Methods
   * Banks
   * Suppliers
   * Contractors
   * Asset Categories
   * Warehouse Categories
   * Expense Categories
   * Revenue Categories
   * Purchase Categories
   * Maintenance Categories
   * Legal Categories
   * CRM Categories
   * Any future lookup or reference data

5. Any new value added by an administrator must automatically become available across all related ERP modules without source-code changes.

6. Support unlimited records throughout the ERP:

   * Customers
   * Leads
   * Suppliers
   * Contractors
   * Projects
   * Buildings
   * Floors
   * Units
   * Reservations
   * Contracts
   * Assets
   * Warehouses
   * Products
   * Materials
   * Documents
   * Transactions
   * Any future business entities

7. All forms, filters, reports, dashboards and searches must dynamically use the centralized configuration engine.

8. Future modules must automatically inherit the same framework.

9. Maintain full backward compatibility with all existing ERP data and modules.

10. Do not create duplicate databases, duplicate entities or isolated data structures.

11. Reuse and extend the existing ERP architecture wherever possible.

12. Ensure complete integration between:

* CRM
* Real Estate
* Finance
* Legal Affairs
* Procurement
* Inventory
* HR
* Engineering
* Customer Service
* Fixed Assets
* Land Bank
* Unit Handover
* All current and future modules

Goal:

Transform the ERP into a scalable, configurable, enterprise-grade platform that can grow for many years without requiring source-code modifications for normal business expansion, new classifications, new categories, new business types or increased operational volume.
